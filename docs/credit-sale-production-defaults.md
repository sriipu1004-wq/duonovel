# Credit sale production defaults

Approved production defaults:

- 5 credits = ¥300
- 8 credits = ¥450
- 12 credits = ¥600
- purchased credits expire after 150 days
- terms version: `2026-09-14-credit-v1`

`LIBREAD_CREDIT_PACK_CATALOG_JSON` and `LIBREAD_CREDIT_TERMS_VERSION` may still override these values, but are no longer required for the approved catalog.

`LIBREAD_CREDIT_PURCHASE_ENABLED` remains the explicit production sale switch and defaults to false.
